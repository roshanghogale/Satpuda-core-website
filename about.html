About Satpuda Core

A software development company from Buldhana, Maharashtra – building custom software for businesses, and our own software for medical stores and hospitals.

Who we are

Satpuda Core Private Limited

Satpuda Core Private Limited is a registered software development company based in Buldhana, Maharashtra. We build websites, Android and iOS apps, Windows and Mac software, and AI automation to order for businesses – and we make our own ready-made software for medical stores and hospitals.

We are a small team, and that is deliberate. When you work with us, you talk directly to the developers who build your software – no dealers, no call centres, no messages passed along.
