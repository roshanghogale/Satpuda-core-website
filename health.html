What is inside

Modules

OPD to IPD, lab to OT, billing to government schemes – switched on for what your hospital actually runs.

Patients & OPD

Registration takes seconds, the queue runs itself, and the doctor ends the consultation with a printed prescription.

  • Registration with UHID, photo, mobile, address, ABHA number / QR, duplicate check and family linking
  • Appointments and doctor-wise tokens – walk-in, advance, emergency, free (camp / staff) and review visits
  • Live OPD queue per doctor with TV calling display, nurse vitals entry and fee status before the doctor starts
  • Consultation: complaints, history, examination, diagnosis quick picks, prescription, lab and radiology orders, advice, follow-up, certificates
  • Unlimited prescription formats – A4, A5 or the doctor's own pad, letterhead or pre-printed, Marathi / Hindi dose words (सकाळी-दुपारी-रात्री)
  • Print the full prescription, medicines only for the chemist, or the test request only for the lab
  • Safety checks: allergy and duplicate-medicine warnings, abnormal lab values flagged, chronic conditions on top of every visit

Patient health record

Every visit, admission, report and medicine of a patient on one timeline.

  • Printable health summary: latest BP, sugar, HbA1c with controlled flags, BMI, conditions, current medicines, allergies
  • Timeline of OPD visits, admissions, lab and radiology reports, surgeries, vaccinations, bills and documents
  • Trend graphs for BP, pulse, weight, sugar, HbA1c, lipids, creatinine and any numeric test; WHO growth charts for children
  • Chronic care register for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid, asthma and CKD with overdue reviews and call buttons
  • Documents: scanned reports, consent forms, certificates and discharge summaries attached to the record
  • Medical store link – prescriptions go to the attached SatpudaCore pharmacy, dispensing comes back

IPD / wards

From bed board to discharge summary, with the final bill built from what was actually done.

  • Bed board: wards, bed types and rates, live occupancy, transfers, ICU / private / general tariffs
  • Admission with consultant, payer, authority letter, attendant, estimate and advance; cashless scheme patients show a red "do not take money" banner
  • Doctor rounds and orders, nurse medicine round with given / missed, vitals charts, intake-output
  • Charges posted automatically: bed and nursing days by rule, doctor visits, procedures, OT, lab, radiology, stock issues
  • Fixed-price packages (normal delivery, LSCS, cataract…) and scheme packages at scheme rates
  • Discharge: summary from templates, final bill, supplementary bill, LAMA / referral / death flows, birth and death registers
  • Consent forms in English, Marathi and Hindi, and estimate letters for CM / PM Relief Fund

Billing & money

Every hospital bills its own way – the rules are settings, not custom code.

  • OPD fee flow: before or after consultation, either, or no fee; block the doctor until paid if you want
  • IPD rules: bed-day counting, suggested advance, daily charges, discharge only after payment, second bill after discharge
  • Service master with different rates per payer and per ward, plus packages and scheme rate lists
  • GST-ready bills, part payments, dues, refunds, cancellations with reason, discounts with permission
  • To collect – one screen of unpaid OPD fees, lab orders and dues
  • Credit payers (company, railway, PSU) grouped into a monthly statement of account
  • Shift-wise and day-wise cash closing by payment mode: expected vs counted

Lab, radiology, OT & casualty

The clinical departments on the same record, with reports on your letterhead.

  • Laboratory: test master with reference ranges by age and sex, worklist, high / low flags, pathologist verification, report print, outsourced tests
  • Radiology: X-ray, sonography, CT and ECG orders with report templates and signed reports
  • Operation theatre: schedule board, surgery booking with team, pre-op checklist, operative notes, implants, OT consumables and charges
  • Emergency / casualty: casualty register, triage colour, outcome, MLC register with police intimation print
  • Statutory registers: birth, death with cause-of-death form, MLC, OPD and IPD – all printable for government submission

Maternity, vaccination & day-care

The programmes a small hospital actually runs, with their due lists.

  • Maternity / ANC: pregnancy register with LMP / EDD, ANC visits, risk flags, high-risk list, delivery record, postnatal visits
  • Vaccination: IAP / government schedule, card print, doses with batch and site, due list by week, parent reminders
  • Day-care: dialysis, chemotherapy and physiotherapy plans with session records and package or per-session billing
  • Reminders for tomorrow's follow-ups, appointments, ANC visits, vaccine dues and lab reports, in Marathi, Hindi or English

Stock, expenses & documents

Consumables, money out, and every letter the hospital has to print.

  • Hospital stock: consumables, OT items, implants and oxygen by location, with batch, expiry and issues posted to the bill
  • Expenses and profit: expense heads, vouchers, salaries, vendor payments, monthly P&L against collections
  • Consent forms (general, surgery, anaesthesia, high-risk, blood, ICU, LAMA, radiology) in three languages
  • Discharge templates per department with medicines, advice, diet, warning signs and follow-up
  • Certificates and letters – fitness, sickness, medical, birth, death, referral, estimates, pre-auth and claims – on your letterhead with numbering

Patient-facing

What the patient sees, without asking them to install anything.

  • Public online booking page per hospital – the patient picks a doctor and slot and gets a token
  • Prescription, lab report and bill links on WhatsApp / SMS, valid 30 days, no app needed
  • Feedback link after the visit or discharge, with ratings on the hospital dashboard
  • TV token display for the waiting hall, with "now calling" chime and hospital notices
  • SMS and WhatsApp templates in Marathi, Hindi and English

Reports & analytics

Today against last month, and the numbers a trust or a bank asks for.

  • Dashboard: patients, new vs old, OPD / IPD, collections, dues, occupancy and lab load with graphs
  • Patients: new by month, area and age, lost follow-ups, repeat rate, top diagnoses and seasonal trends
  • Doctors: consultations, procedures, collections and share; referrers and commission
  • Money: day book, payment modes, discounts and refunds with reasons, dues, insurance receivables and ageing, expenses and profit
  • Clinical: IPD occupancy and average stay, OT volumes, lab turnaround, maternity outcomes, vaccination coverage

ABDM

Where we stand on ABDM

Written plainly, because "ABDM compliant" means a certification, not a feature list.

Working today

  • ABHA number and ABHA QR scan on the patient record
  • Records kept in ABDM-ready formats (FHIR)
  • MJPJAY / PM-JAY package codes and CGHS 2025 rates

In certification – M1 to M4

Sandbox registration submitted 10 October 2026.

  • M1: ABHA creation and verification at the counter
  • M2: hospital records linked to the patient's ABHA (HIP)
  • M3: other hospitals' records with the patient's consent (HIU)
  • M4: Scan & Share QR registration, HFR / HPR registration
  • NHA certification and the “ABDM enabled” listing follow the sandbox stage

Also on the way: NHCX for direct pre-authorisation and claims with insurers, UHI so your doctors and slots can be found from any patient app, and e-prescriptions flowing straight into a SatpudaCore pharmacy.
