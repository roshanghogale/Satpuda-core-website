What you get

All of this is in the one-time price – there is no feature locked behind a yearly plan.

Billing & sales

A GST bill in seconds, from the keyboard, the phone, the browser or your voice.

  • GST tax invoice with HSN, GST % per line, item and bill discount, round-off to the paisa
  • Bill numbers per financial year – never two bills with one number, even from two devices offline
  • Counter sale for walk-in customers; a named customer gets their own bill
  • Autosave – a power cut or a closed window loses nothing, and the bill is never duplicated
  • Several bills open at once in tabs
  • Regular medicines – type the customer's name and their usual medicines come up in one tap
  • Voice billing on PC – say the medicine and quantity, the bill fills itself
  • Cash, online, or part now and the rest on due; previous due and credit printed on the bill
  • Schedule H / H1 – a scheduled medicine asks for the doctor and patient; selling below cost is warned
  • Sales history searchable by bill, customer, phone, medicine or batch

Purchases

Supplier bills go in fast, and stock and supplier dues follow by themselves.

  • Purchase entry with batch, expiry, MRP, rate, GST, free quantity and discounts
  • New medicines created straight from the purchase line
  • Bill photo / PDF import – photograph the supplier's invoice and check the read lines (Pro)
  • The same supplier invoice number cannot be entered twice
  • Supplier payments (cash / online / part) with a running due
  • Purchase history by supplier, bill number, medicine or batch, with a safe edit guard

Inventory & stock

Every strip counted, batch by batch, with nothing to recount.

  • Stock by batch and expiry, moved only by bills, purchases, returns and write-offs
  • Medicine master of 3.9 lakh medicines – name, company, pack, HSN, GST, schedule
  • Low-stock and near-expiry alerts when the app opens
  • Show filter: Active, Hidden, Out of stock, Expired or All, with counts
  • Edit once, everywhere – correcting a medicine's schedule, HSN or GST also corrects its old bills, so the Schedule H register stays right
  • Rack, section and box location for each medicine
  • Reorder list from low stock, grouped by supplier
  • General (non-medicine) products on the same bill
  • Nightly stock check on the server against every movement

Customers, suppliers & dues

Know who owes what, and what you owe, at any moment.

  • Udhari ledger – every bill, payment and return, with due and advance credit worked out for you
  • Customer payments in cash, online or part; the due on the next bill updates at once
  • Supplier ledger and payments, with the due before and after each payment
  • Customer GSTIN kept for B2B bills
  • Doctors list for prescriptions on scheduled medicines
  • Nightly dues check on the server against the ledger

Returns & write-offs

Goods come back, and the books follow on their own.

  • Sales return against the bill, as a refund or as credit to the customer's account; stock comes back
  • Purchase return to the supplier with a supplier-wise print; the supplier's due follows
  • Expired or damaged write-off with a reason, recorded and taken out of stock

Reports & GST

Returns-ready GST figures that match the printed bills to the paisa.

  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and HSN summary, plus sales and purchase GST registers
  • Schedule H / H1 register (standard or laser layout, with a pharmacist sign column) for drug inspector checks
  • Sales, purchase, returns and payment reports by day, month, financial year or custom dates
  • Home dashboard: today's sales, cash vs online, dues, low stock, expiring soon
  • Export to Excel, CSV or PDF

Printing

Prints on what your shop already has, aligned bill after bill.

  • Laser / inkjet: A4, A5 and A6 bills, two copies or one per page, black-only mode to save colour ink
  • Dot matrix slips (e.g. Epson LX-310) on 4-inch or 6-inch paper that stay aligned; Print All for a batch
  • Silent printing – the bill prints straight away, with no dialog
  • Save as PDF and share; the phone prints through Android

Works without internet

The shop never stops when the net goes.

  • Offline-first – the PC and the phone each keep their own copy and save every bill at once
  • Syncs by itself when the net is back, with a Synced / Sending / No internet badge
  • Nothing lost, nothing doubled – each device has its own block of bill numbers, so two devices selling offline still end with the exact stock
  • If two people edit the same record offline, both versions are kept and the owner is told
  • An existing shop switches over by itself on the first start of the new version

Web login & staff permissions

Run the shop from any browser, with a separate login for every person.

  • Web app at medical.satpudacore.online: billing and print, history, purchases, stock, dues and reports
  • One ID and password per person – the owner and each staff member
  • Permissions per staff member: billing, history (view / edit / delete), returns, purchases, stock, dues, reports, settings
  • The owner adds staff, sets permissions, resets passwords and disables a login
  • Every bill and change records who made it
  • Passwords stored only as hashes, sessions end when idle, a login locks after repeated wrong passwords
  • The web needs internet; the PC and phone apps are the ones that work offline

Backup, multi-store & setup

Your data in three places, and a new shop running in minutes.

  • Server copy of your shop with nightly backups
  • Google Drive backup and USB backup / restore, with a guard that refuses a restore that would lose newer bills
  • Several stores on one PC, each with its own data
  • Medical store or veterinary / pet shop mode
  • One-click installer for Windows 10 / 11; updates arrive inside the app
  • Android opening-stock loader to enter a new shop's starting stock quickly
